🛡️ Privacy & Technical Honesty First

WaySafe is built on transparent, privacy-centric engineering principles:

  • No fabricated numbers: Battery percentages and GPS coordinates reflect true device sensors or graceful browser fallbacks.
  • Consensual tracking: We do not claim web apps can track your phone continuously in the background or toggle your phone's hardware battery-saver mode. We provide actionable guides for you to do so safely.
  • Verified delivery: We generate real pre-composed device SMS and phone calls directly via native device protocols.
